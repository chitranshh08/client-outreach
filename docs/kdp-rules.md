# KDP Rules & Numbers

The single reference for KDP facts used across this repo. KDP changes its terms, so
anything tagged **(verify)** must be checked against KDP Help before money or a
publish depends on it. Update this file when something changes, with the date.

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-29_

## Royalties & pricing

**Kindle ebook**
- 70% royalty plan: list price **$2.99–$9.99** (US) and delivery fee applies
  (about $0.15/MB in the US **(verify)**). Keep images compressed.
- 35% royalty plan: outside that price band.
- Default pricing for this catalog: non-fiction $4.99–$6.99; series book 1 can go
  lower ($2.99–$3.99) to feed read-through.

**Paperback**
- Royalty = 60% of list price − printing cost (Amazon marketplaces). Expanded
  Distribution pays 40% − printing cost.
- Printing cost depends on page count, ink and marketplace: always run KDP's
  printing cost calculator **(verify)**.
- Default pricing: set so royalty ≥ $3.00; typical non-fiction 6×9 at $12.99–$16.99.
- A paperback at a higher price makes the ebook look like a deal. Always publish both.

## KDP Select (Kindle Unlimited)
- Applies to the **ebook only**. Requires 90-day exclusivity (the ebook can't be sold on
  Apple, Kobo, Google and so on, including excerpts over 10% on your own site).
- Earns KENP page-read royalties and unlocks either Kindle Countdown Deals or Free Book
  Promotion (up to 5 free days per 90-day term).
- Auto-renews every 90 days. Turn off renewal if we decide to go wide.
- Decision rule: enroll if a meaningful share of the top 20 competitors show the KU
  badge. Skip if the niche's top sellers are mostly wide/non-KU.

## Metadata limits
- **Title + subtitle**: combined under 200 characters. The subtitle is where search
  phrases go naturally. The title stays clean and memorable.
- **Description**: up to 4,000 characters, limited HTML (bold, italics, headings, lists).
- **Keywords**: 7 slots, up to 50 characters each. Use phrases buyers actually type
  (Amazon autocomplete). Don't repeat words already in the title or categories.
- **Categories**: choose up to 3 per format.
- **Series**: link books via KDP's series feature so the series page appears.
- **Author**: pen name exactly as in `pen-names/`. Set up Author Central for each pen name.

## Content & AI rules
- **AI disclosure**: KDP asks whether text, images or translations are AI-generated.
  Content generated by AI counts as AI-generated **even if heavily edited**, so our
  manuscripts are disclosed as AI-generated text. AI-assisted (a human wrote it, AI
  helped edit) doesn't need disclosure.
- KDP rejects or removes "disappointing content": misleading titles/covers, content
  mostly freely available online, very thin books, duplicated content, or poor
  formatting. Our quality bar exists to stay far away from this.
- KDP limits how many new titles one account can publish per day (currently 3 **(verify)**).
- No trademarks, brand names, or other authors' names in title/subtitle/keywords unless
  it's truly the book's subject and allowed.
- Metadata must not contain: "bestseller", "free", "#1", promotional pricing claims,
  or anything misleading.

## Reviews (Amazon Community Guidelines)
Allowed:
- A neutral request in the back matter ("If this helped, an honest review helps other
  readers find it").
- **Advance review copies**: a free copy given before or at launch, with no requirement
  to review and no requirement that reviews be positive.
- Free promo days (KDP Select) to widen readership.

Forbidden (can get the account terminated):
- Paying or rewarding for reviews in any form, including gift cards and refunds.
- Review swaps or review groups between authors.
- Reviews from family, close friends, or anyone with a financial interest.
- Asking for positive or 5-star reviews, or only asking happy readers (review gating).
- Reviewing competitors' books.

## Files & formatting
- Ebook upload: EPUB or DOCX (or KPF from Kindle Create). MOBI isn't accepted for new
  reflowable ebooks.
- Ebook cover: JPG/TIFF, ideal **2560 × 1600 px** (height × width, 1.6:1).
- Paperback: common trim **6×9 in** (non-fiction) or 5.5×8.5 in. Interior PDF with
  embedded fonts. The cover is a full wrap (back + spine + front) sized with KDP's cover
  calculator, including 0.125 in bleed. Spine text only if ≥ 100 pages **(verify)**.
- Always check with Kindle Previewer (ebook) and the KDP print previewer (paperback).

## Upload checklist (owner does this)
1. Pen name + series set correctly.
2. Title/subtitle/description/keywords/categories pasted from `listing.md`.
3. AI disclosure answered honestly.
4. KDP Select decision applied (ebook).
5. Territories: worldwide rights. Prices per marketplace set (auto-convert from USD,
   then round to .99).
6. Previewer checked on phone + Kindle + tablet views.
7. Update `tracker/catalog.csv` with status `in-review`, then `live` + ASIN.
