# Quality Bar: what "perfect book" means here

Reviews and read-through come from books that **deliver the promise fast and completely**.
A book ships only when it passes every section below.

## 1. Promise
- [ ] One clear promise that fits in one sentence ("Fall asleep in 20 minutes without pills").
- [ ] Title and subtitle state that promise in words buyers actually search for.
- [ ] The book delivers exactly that promise: no bait, no padding.

## 2. Reader experience (this is what earns reviews)
- [ ] **Quick win in the first 10%**: the reader gets something useful before the Kindle
      "Look Inside" sample ends. The sample sells the book.
- [ ] Intro ≤ 1,500 words: who it's for, what they'll get, how to use the book. No life story.
- [ ] Every chapter ends with an action step, checklist, or exercise.
- [ ] Includes practical extras: templates, scripts, trackers, a quick-start summary.
- [ ] Beats the competition: covers the top complaints found in competitors' 1–3★ reviews
      (logged in `brief.md`).
- [ ] Length fits the niche: non-fiction usually 20k–35k words. Never padded to hit a count.

## 3. Writing
- [ ] Follows the pen name's voice (`pen-names/<name>.md`).
- [ ] No AI tells (see list in CLAUDE.md). Varied sentence length, specific examples.
- [ ] Every fact, stat and study is real and cited (endnotes or inline). Anything that
      can't be verified is cut.
- [ ] Examples labeled as illustrative/composite unless real.
- [ ] Proofread: no typos, repeated sections, or chapter-to-chapter contradictions.
- [ ] Owner's human edit pass done (reads it fully, adds their own touches).

## 4. Packaging
- [ ] Cover matches the category's top-sellers and reads clearly as a thumbnail (~150 px wide).
- [ ] Description: hook → pain → promise → bullet benefits → who it's for → call to action.
- [ ] Clean formatting: working clickable TOC, consistent headings, no stray symbols,
      checked on phone view in Kindle Previewer.

## 5. Back matter (read-through + reviews + audience)
- [ ] Short thank-you plus a neutral, honest-review request with a direct link.
- [ ] "Next in the series" page with a hook and link (or "coming soon" + social to follow).
- [ ] Pen name's social handle(s) and what readers get by following.
- [ ] Short author bio (pen name, no fake credentials).

## Review-maximizing principles (legit only)
1. **Put the review ask right after the payoff**, not before it. The best spot is right
   after the final action plan.
2. **Remove friction**: a direct link to the review page per marketplace where possible.
3. **Advance review copies** go to real readers from the pen name's social audience,
   with no strings attached.
4. **Satisfied readers review**: the fastest way to more reviews is a better book, so
   mine competitors' negative reviews and fix every complaint.
5. **Series read-through** turns one satisfied reader into 3–5 sales.
