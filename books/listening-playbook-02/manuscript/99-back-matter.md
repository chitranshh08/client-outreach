# The 30 Scripts Cheat Sheet

*Short versions of every script in Part 2. Photocopy these pages or take a photo for your
phone. Always start with the One Clear Ask: get close, get their eyes, say it short, wait,
follow through.*

**When they tune you out (Chapter 4)** *Walk over first.*

1. **Mid-play:** "That's a big sticker picture. Coat on, please."
2. **Come here:** "Time to come inside, please. Snack is ready."
3. **"In a minute!":** "One more page, then teeth." Then: "Teeth now, please."
4. **Stop that:** "Feet on the floor, please. Jumping is for outside."
5. **One parent, not the other:** "I asked you. I'll help you do it."
6. **"Can I? Can I?":** "The answer is no for today. Asked and answered."

**No, defiance and power struggles (Chapter 5)** *Don't pick up the rope.*

7. **"No!":** "Coat on. Do you want to zip it yourself, or should I?"
8. **"You can't make me!":** "You're right. I can't make you want to. Teeth now, then we'll read."
9. **Running off:** Near roads, hold hands first. In safe places: "Freeze! Come back to me, please."
10. **Doing it while watching you:** "Cup stays on the table. If it drops again, the cup goes away."
11. **"But why?":** One short reason, once. "I told you why. Teeth now, please."
12. **Breaking a rule:** "Rule check: balls are for outside. Outside or in the basket?"

**Cleanup and chores (Chapter 6)** *One step at a time.*

13. **Toy cleanup:** "Cars in the red bin, please." Then: "Now books on the shelf."
14. **"I can't do it!":** "You do the first part, and I'll help with the rest."
15. **Dawdling:** "Socks." Wait. "Now shirt." Wait. "Now pants."
16. **Bath time:** "Ducks or the boat?" Getting out: "Two more splashes, then the plug comes out."
17. **Helping out:** "Table captain! A spoon by every plate, please."

**Mealtimes (Chapter 7)** *You decide what, when and where. They decide how much.*

18. **Coming to the table:** "Dinner's on the table. Come sit with us, please."
19. **Staying seated:** "Bottom on the chair. Are you still eating, or are you done?"
20. **"Yuck!":** "You don't have to eat it. In our family we say 'No, thank you.'"
21. **Dropping food:** "Food stays on the plate. Are you done, or still eating?"
22. **Please and thank you:** "Try that again with 'please.'"

**Whining, interrupting and rude words (Chapter 8)** *Answer the better way, every time.*

23. **Whining:** "I can't understand the whiny voice. Can you ask in your regular voice?"
24. **Interrupting:** "I'm talking to Grandma. Hand on my arm, please."
25. **Potty words:** "Potty words are for the bathroom."
26. **"You're stupid!":** "That's a hurtful word. Try that again in a kind way."
27. **Eye-rolls:** Let it go. "Backpack on the hook, please."

**Trickier moments (Chapter 9)** *Plan ahead.*

28. **Someone else's house:** "At Nia's house, toys stay in the playroom, please."
29. **The store:** "You can look with your eyes. Hands on the cart, please."
30. **"I didn't do it!":** "I see crayon on the wall. Let's clean it together."

**When they still don't:** One reminder, with a choice. Helping hands, or "When… then…" A
calm consequence: related, small, soon. Then move on.
