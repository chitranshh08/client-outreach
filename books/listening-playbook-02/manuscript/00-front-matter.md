# Find Your Moment

*Asked three times already? Find your situation below and go straight to that page.*

**When they tune you out** (Chapter 4)

- Ignoring you in the middle of play ("Did you hear me?")
- "Come here" and time to come inside
- "In a minute!"
- Asking them to stop (jumping, banging, poking)
- Listens to one parent but not the other
- "Can I? Can I? Can I?"

**No, defiance and power struggles** (Chapter 5)

- The flat "No!"
- "You can't make me!"
- Running off when you call
- Doing it while looking right at you
- "But why?"
- Breaking a rule you just set

**Cleanup and chores** (Chapter 6)

- Toy cleanup
- "I can't do it!" (when they can)
- The 20-minute dawdle
- Bath time: getting in, and getting out
- Helping around the house

**Mealtimes** (Chapter 7)

- Won't come to the table
- Won't stay in the seat
- "Yuck! I'm not eating that!"
- Playing with or dropping food
- Forgetting please and thank you

**Whining, interrupting and rude words** (Chapter 8)

- The whiny voice
- Interrupting when you're on the phone or talking
- Potty words and silly-rude talk
- "You're stupid!" and "Go away!"
- Eye-rolls and back-talk

**Trickier moments** (Chapter 9)

- Not listening at a friend's house or a playdate
- Touching everything in a store
- "I didn't do it!"

**Need it right now?** Turn to the One Clear Ask in Chapter 1.
