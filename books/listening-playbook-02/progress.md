## Continuity (read before drafting the next chapter)

**Defined terms (use exactly these names):**
- **One Clear Ask**: 5 steps: Get close · Get their eyes · Say it short · Wait · Follow through (Ch 1). Say it short = tell, don't ask; what to do, not what to stop; one thing at a time; ≤ 10 words. Wait = count to five in your head, don't repeat.
- **Follow-Through Ladder**: Rung 1 One reminder, with a choice (the only repeat; give the warning here if a consequence may follow) · Rung 2 Helping hands, or "When… then…" · Rung 3 A calm consequence · Rung 4 Move on (Ch 3)
- **helping hands**: doing it *with* them, not for them; "Your hands or my hands?"
- **"When… then…"**: *when*, not *if*; "then" = what was going to happen anyway (no new prize)
- **related, small, soon**: the consequence rule (Ch 3; sourced to AACAP S8)
- **Consequence Picker**: printable in Ch 3
- **Keep the follow-through, drop the yell**: the core insight (Intro, Ch 2)
- **the repeat loop**: ask, ask, ask, yell, they move (Intro, Ch 1, Ch 2)
- **positive opposite** (Kazdin, S7) and **catch them listening** (Ch 3)
- **reward vs bribe**: reward = agreed before, when calm; bribe = offered mid-standoff (Ch 3)
- **connection before direction**: one sentence joining their world before the ask (Ch 1 step 2, Ch 2)
- **Two times to skip the ladder**: safety (act first) and meltdown (feelings first, task later; Book 1's 3-Line Script) (Ch 3)
- **Your 5-Minute Action**: heading for every chapter's end action (same as Book 1)
- Script card for Part 2 (always this order): What's going on · **Say this** · **If they still don't** · Instead of / Try · Age tweaks (2–3 · 4–5 · 6–7) · Next time
