## Continuity (read before drafting the next chapter)
**Defined terms (exact names):**
- **2-Minute Unspiral**: Notice · Name it · Solve or park · One small step (Ch 1)
- **Three spirals**: the **Replay** (past) · the **What-If** (future) · the **Can't-Decide** (a choice) (Ch 1); what each wants: Replay = one lesson + permission to let go; What-If = plan for likely + park the rest; Can't-Decide = deadline + good enough (Ch 2)
- **Solve or Park** question: "Is there one thing I can do about this in the next 24 hours?" (Ch 1)
- **Worry Window**: 15–30 min, fixed time and place, not in bed; "Not now. That's for the window." (Ch 3, S3)
- **Eight tools** (Ch 3): 1 Worry Window · 2 Solve or Park · 3 Name It · 4 Thought Label ("I'm having the thought that…"; word-repetition variation) · 5 Brain Dump (list, not a diary; at night a specific to-do list) · 6 Best–Worst–Likely · 7 Good-Enough Rule (≤ 3 must-haves, a real deadline, first option that fits) · 8 Change the Channel (10–20 min absorbing task; scrolling doesn't count)
- **Tool Chooser** (Ch 3 printable)
- Concrete "how" vs abstract "why" thinking (S1); thought suppression backfires (S4)
- Chapter-end heading: **One Small Step**
- Part 2 card (always this order): The spiral (type) · What's going on · **Try this** · **Say this to yourself** · If it keeps going · One small step
