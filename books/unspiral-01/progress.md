## Continuity (read before drafting the next chapter)
**Defined terms (exact names):**
- **2-Minute Unspiral**: Notice · Name it · Solve or park · One small step (Ch 1)
- **Three spirals**: the **Replay** (past) · the **What-If** (future) · the **Can't-Decide** (a choice) (Ch 1); what each wants: Replay = one lesson + permission to let go; What-If = plan for likely + park the rest; Can't-Decide = deadline + good enough (Ch 2)
- **Solve or Park** question: "Is there one thing I can do about this in the next 24 hours?" (Ch 1)
