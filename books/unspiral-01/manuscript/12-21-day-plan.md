# Chapter 11: Your 21-Day Unspiral Plan

You now have everything in this book: the 2-Minute Unspiral, eight tools, and 27 moments,
each with the words to say and a step to take.

That's a lot to hold. The easiest way to lose it is to try everything at once for two days,
then slip back into the old loops. So this chapter turns the book into a simple three-week
plan: **one small action a day, building from noticing to handling your hardest moments.**

## How the plan works

- **Each day has one action,** usually ten minutes or less. Some take one minute.
- **Missed a day? Just carry on.** Don't restart. The plan works even when you do it
  imperfectly. (And if you catch yourself spiraling about missing a day, that's a Replay.
  You know what to do.)
- **Keep your tally from Chapter 1.** One mark for every spiral you catch. Catching more in
  week one is a good sign: it means you're noticing.
- **Estimate on Day 1 and Day 21.** A rough guess is fine: *About how many minutes did I
  spend stuck in loops yesterday?* Write the number in the tracker at the end of this
  chapter.

---

## Week 1 (Days 1 to 7): Notice and name

*Focus: the 2-Minute Unspiral (Chapter 1) and the three spirals (Chapter 2)*

- **Day 1:** Estimate yesterday's loop time and write it down. Start your tally. No
  changes yet. Just notice.
- **Day 2:** Say **"I'm spiraling"** every time you catch a loop, silently or out loud.
  That's the whole task.
- **Day 3:** Name each spiral as you catch it: Replay, What-If or Can't-Decide. Next to
  each tally mark, write R, W or C.
- **Day 4:** Run the full 2-Minute Unspiral on at least one spiral: notice, name it, solve
  or park, one small step.
- **Day 5:** Practice the solve-or-park question. For one loop, write down the answer to
  **"Is there one thing I can do about this in the next 24 hours?"** Then do it, or park it.
- **Day 6:** Set up your Worry Window (Chapter 3). Pick a time and a place that isn't your
  bed, and put it in your calendar for every day of the next two weeks.
- **Day 7:** Look at your letters from Day 3 onward. Which spiral shows up most?

**Check-in:** Am I catching spirals sooner than I did a week ago? Which spiral is mine?

---

## Week 2 (Days 8 to 14): Park it, solve it, use the tools

*Focus: the Worry Window, Solve or Park, and the eight tools (Chapter 3)*

From today, run your Worry Window every day. During the day, write worries down and say
**"Not now. That's for the window."**

- **Day 8:** Your first full week of windows starts. In the window today, go through the list
  and sort each worry: solve or park?
- **Day 9:** Use the Thought Label on your harshest thought: **"I'm having the thought
  that…"**
- **Day 10:** Run Best–Worst–Likely on paper for one What-If you're carrying right now.
- **Day 11:** Do a bedtime Brain Dump (Chapter 8). A specific to-do list, five minutes,
  then close the notebook.
- **Day 12:** Write a "change the channel" list: three absorbing things you can do for ten
  to twenty minutes when a loop won't quit. Keep it in your phone's notes.
- **Day 13:** Make one "check later" rule (Chapter 5): a time to stop checking messages, a
  set time to look at a chat, or a muted thread.
- **Day 14:** Set up your night kit (Chapter 8): notebook and pen by the bed, clock turned
  away, phone out of reach.

**Check-in:** Is the Worry Window staying inside its time? (If not, see Chapter 10, snag 1.)
Which tool have I used most this week?

---

## Week 3 (Days 15 to 21): Your moments

*Focus: the moment cards (Chapters 4 to 9) and the Good-Enough Rule (Chapter 7)*

- **Day 15:** Pick your three most common moments from the **Find Your Spiral** list at the
  front of the book. Write their bold lines on a card or in your phone's notes.
- **Day 16:** Use one of your three cards the next time that moment comes up.
- **Day 17:** Use the Good-Enough Rule on one small decision: up to three must-haves, a
  short deadline, the first option that fits.
- **Day 18:** Make one medium decision you've been putting off. Set a deadline of tonight.
- **Day 19:** Write down the tool that has helped you most (you picked one in Chapter 10).
  That's your first move from now on. Put it at the top of your card.
- **Day 20:** Build your own card for a moment that isn't in this book, using the blank
  card at the end of this chapter.
- **Day 21:** Estimate yesterday's loop time again and write it next to your Day 1 number.

---

## Your Day 21 check-in

Compare your Day 1 and Day 21 numbers, then answer honestly. There are no wrong answers.

1. Do my spirals **end sooner** than they did three weeks ago?
2. Am I **catching them earlier**, before they've run for an hour?
3. Which **spiral** is still the hardest?
4. Which **tool** do I reach for first now?
5. Which **moment** still catches me? (Go back to its card, or check Chapter 10.)

If your loops are even a little shorter, that's real progress. A spiral that used to run
all evening and now runs for twenty minutes is twenty minutes closer to quiet. You don't
need to stop thinking. You need to keep finding the exit, a little faster each time.

Keep the tools that work and drop the ones that don't. When a new kind of spiral shows up,
come back to this book, find the closest moment, and use its card.

And if, after three weeks of steady effort, the loops are getting longer instead of shorter,
or worry is affecting your sleep, work or relationships, look at the signs in Chapter 10
and talk to your doctor. Asking for help is part of the plan, not a step outside it.
