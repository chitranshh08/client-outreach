# Find Your Spiral

*Stuck right now? Find your moment below and go straight to that page.*

**The Replay** (Chapter 4)

- The conversation you keep replaying
- "Why did I say that?"
- The message you already sent
- A mistake at work
- The argument that ended badly
- Replaying the party on the way home

**Texts, silences and "what did they mean?"** (Chapter 5)

- The unanswered text
- "Seen" and no reply
- The short reply: "ok."
- Waiting to hear back
- Silence after you posted something

**The What-Ifs** (Chapter 6)

- Before a big event or presentation
- "What if I lose my job?"
- Worrying about someone you love
- Money what-ifs
- Health worries and the search spiral
- Plans that might go wrong

**Can't-Decide** (Chapter 7)

- Small choices that eat an hour
- Saying yes or no to plans
- A big decision
- Second-guessing a decision you already made
- Asking everyone and still not knowing

**The Night Loop** (Chapter 8)

- Can't fall asleep because your brain won't stop
- Waking at 3 a.m. and starting the list
- Replaying the day in bed

**The People Spirals** (Chapter 9)

- "What do they think of me?"
- Comparing yourself online

**Need it right now?** Turn to the 2-Minute Unspiral in Chapter 1.
