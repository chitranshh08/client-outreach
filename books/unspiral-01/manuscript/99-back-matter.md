# The 27 Moments Cheat Sheet

*Every moment in Part 2 in one line: the spiral, the tool, and what to say to yourself.
Take a photo of these pages for your phone. Always start with the 2-Minute Unspiral:
notice, name it, solve or park, one small step.*

**The Replay (Chapter 4)** *One lesson, then let it go.*

1. **The conversation you keep replaying:** Name It, one lesson. "I've got the lesson. I don't need the rerun."
2. **"Why did I say that?":** Swap "why" for "what". "It's louder in my head than it was in the room."
3. **The message you already sent:** One reread, then decide. "It's sent. It's fine. It's not mine to reread."
4. **A mistake at work:** Fix it, say it once, write the lesson. "I fixed what I could. The rest is a replay."
5. **The argument that ended badly:** One repair message. "I don't need the perfect comeback. I need one next step."
6. **Replaying the party on the way home:** Make the review fair. "Tired brains write bad reviews. I'll read it again in the morning."

**Texts, silences and "what did they mean?" (Chapter 5)** *List the other stories.*

7. **The unanswered text:** Best–Worst–Likely, then wait. "No reply isn't a reply. It's just no information yet."
8. **"Seen" and no reply:** Three other stories, then mute. "I'm writing a story with no facts in it."
9. **The short reply: "ok.":** One honest check, once. "Short isn't the same as upset."
10. **Waiting to hear back:** Worry Window and an "if it's a no" plan. "Waiting isn't a job. I don't have to do it all day."
11. **Silence after you posted something:** Solve or park, then close the app. "Quiet isn't a verdict."

**The What-Ifs (Chapter 6)** *Plan for likely. Park the rest.*

12. **Before a big event or presentation:** Turn each fear into a task. "I've prepared for likely. That's enough."
13. **"What if I lose my job?":** Write the first three steps. "If it happens, I know my first step. Until then, it hasn't happened."
14. **Worrying about someone you love:** Agree on a check-in. "Worrying isn't protecting them. The plan is."
15. **Money what-ifs:** Look at the real number. "Numbers I can see are easier than numbers I imagine."
16. **Health worries and the search spiral:** One call, no searching. "A search can't examine me. A doctor can."
17. **Plans that might go wrong:** Three backups for the likeliest problems. "I have a backup for likely. I'll handle the rest if it happens."

**Can't-Decide (Chapter 7)** *A deadline and "good enough".*

18. **Small choices that eat an hour:** Good-Enough Rule, deadline in minutes. "Good enough, chosen, beats perfect, still searching."
19. **Saying yes or no to plans:** A time to answer and a gut check. "A clear answer is kinder than a late maybe."
20. **A big decision:** Research on purpose, three must-haves, a real deadline. "I can decide well without deciding perfectly."
21. **Second-guessing a decision you already made:** Reread your must-haves. "I made a good choice with what I knew. I'm done choosing."
22. **Asking everyone and still not knowing:** One or two people, one specific question. "More opinions won't make it feel safe. A deadline will make it done."

**The Night Loop (Chapter 8)** *Get it on paper. Save it for tomorrow.*

23. **Can't fall asleep because your brain won't stop:** Bedtime Brain Dump. "It's on the list. I don't have to hold it."
24. **Waking at 3 a.m. and starting the list:** One line in the notebook, then something dull to hold. "Nothing gets solved at 3 a.m. It'll be smaller in the morning."
25. **Replaying the day in bed:** Close the day before bed. "I already reviewed today. Bed is for sleeping."

**The People Spirals (Chapter 9)** *You can't see inside their heads.*

26. **"What do they think of me?":** Name It, Thought Label, then listen outward. "Most people are thinking about themselves, not me."
27. **Comparing yourself online:** Close the app, change the channel, set a limit. "I'm comparing my whole life to their best photo."

**When it keeps coming back:** Run the 2-Minute Unspiral again. It's often faster the second
time. And if worry is affecting your daily life, see Chapter 10.
