## The script card (repeated format for every Part 2 moment)

```
### Moment 12: "We have to leave the playground"
What's going on: one sentence on the child's brain/need.
SAY THIS:  "You wish we could stay. It's hard to leave fun things. Two more slides, then shoes."
IF THEY ESCALATE:  the follow-up script + what you physically do
INSTEAD OF → TRY:  "Stop crying or we're never coming back" → "You can be sad and still come with me."
AGE TWEAKS:  2–3 · 4–5 · 6–7
PREVENT IT NEXT TIME:  one sentence
```

## Content rules for this book
- Every factual claim about child development cites a real, checkable source
  (Harvard Center on the Developing Child, AAP/HealthyChildren.org, CDC milestones,
  peer-reviewed studies) in `sources.md`. Anything that can't be verified gets cut.
- Disclaimer in the front matter: general educational information, not medical or
  psychological advice; see a pediatrician for concerns about development, safety or frequency.
- Examples are illustrative composites ("Imagine Mia, age 4…"), labeled as such once in the intro.
- No diagnostic claims (ADHD, autism, sensory processing disorder). Chapter 10 only says
  *when* to talk to a professional.
