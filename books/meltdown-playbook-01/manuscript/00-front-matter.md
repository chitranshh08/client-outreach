# Find Your Moment

*In the middle of a meltdown? Find your situation below and go straight to that page.*

**Everyday battles** (Chapter 4)

- "Five more minutes!" Leaving the park or a playdate
- "But I'm not done!" Turning off screens
- "I'm not wearing that!" Getting dressed
- "I wanted the BLUE cup!" The tiny thing that isn't tiny
- "Why can't I have a cookie?" Hearing no
- "I can't wait!" Waiting in line, waiting for you

**Hitting, biting and throwing** (Chapter 5)

- When they hit you
- When they hit another child
- When they bite
- When toys start flying

**Public meltdowns** (Chapter 6)

- The checkout-lane candy standoff
- Meltdown at a restaurant
- The playground scene
- Family gatherings (and Grandma's "In my day…")
- The car-seat arch-and-scream

**Mornings and bedtime** (Chapter 7)

- "I don't want to get up!"
- Shoes, coat, teeth: the morning rush
- "Just one more story!"
- "I'm not tired!"
- "There's a monster in my room"

**Siblings and sharing** (Chapter 8)

- The toy grab
- "It's not fair!"
- When siblings hit each other
- Jealous of the new baby

**Big disappointments, big fears, big words** (Chapter 9)

- Losing a game
- The broken toy (or broken tower)
- When plans get cancelled
- Tears at daycare drop-off
- Scared of the dark, dogs or the doctor
- "I hate you!"

**Need it right now?** Turn to the 3-Line Meltdown Script in Chapter 1.
