# Chapter 13: Your 30-Day Calmer Home Plan

You've now got everything this book has to offer: the 60-Second Reset, the 3-Line Script, the
Calm Words Formula, 30 scripts for real moments, and the tools to teach calm and repair
mistakes.

That's a lot. And when there's a lot, it's easy to try everything at once for three days,
feel overwhelmed, and go back to old habits.

So this chapter turns the whole book into a simple plan: **one small focus each week, for
four weeks.** You don't need to be perfect. You just need to keep going. By the end of the
month, the new words won't feel like a script anymore. They'll feel like you.

## How the plan works

- **Each week has one focus.** Only one. Everything else can wait.
- **Each day has one small action**, usually five minutes or less.
- **Missed a day? Just carry on.** Don't restart, don't give up. The plan works even if you
  do it imperfectly.
- **Check in at the end of each week.** A few questions help you see your progress, even when
  it's slow.

Keep your meltdown tracker from Chapter 2 going through the month. At the end, you'll be
able to compare Week 1 with Week 4.

---

## Week 1: Calm yourself first

*Focus: the 60-Second Reset and the 3-Line Script (Chapter 1)*

- **Day 1:** Practice the 60-Second Reset twice while calm: feet, breathe out, soft face, low voice.
- **Day 2:** Write the 3-Line Script where you'll see it: fridge, phone lock screen, car.
- **Day 3:** Use the reset once during a real hard moment, even a small one.
- **Day 4:** Use the 3-Line Script at the next meltdown. Then stay close and quiet.
- **Day 5:** Say one of your own feelings out loud, calmly: "I'm frustrated. I'm taking a breath."
- **Day 6:** Use the script again. Notice one thing that went a little better.
- **Day 7: Check-in.** Did I stay calmer at least once this week? Which step of the reset helps
  me most?

---

## Week 2: Your top three flashpoints

*Focus: the scripts for the moments that happen most in your home (Chapters 3 to 9)*

- **Day 8:** Look at your meltdown tracker. Pick the three moments that happen most.
- **Day 9:** Find those three moments in the Find Your Moment list. Read their **Say this** lines
  out loud, twice.
- **Day 10:** Use the script for flashpoint number one.
- **Day 11:** Use the script for flashpoint number two.
- **Day 12:** Use the script for flashpoint number three.
- **Day 13:** For any moment that isn't in the book, write your own script using the Calm Words
  Formula fill-in card (Chapter 3).
- **Day 14: Check-in.** Are any of the three flashpoints shorter or less frequent? Which script
  feels most natural?

---

## Week 3: Prevent the next storm

*Focus: routines, warnings and the basics (Chapters 2, 4, 7 and 10)*

- **Day 15:** Check HALT. Plan snacks before the hardest time of day.
- **Day 16:** Start giving concrete warnings before transitions: "Three more slides, then shoes."
- **Day 17:** Make a picture routine chart for mornings or bedtime (Chapter 7).
- **Day 18:** Use the chart: "What's next on the chart?" instead of giving orders.
- **Day 19:** Move bedtime 15 minutes earlier if mornings or evenings are rough.
- **Day 20:** Give each child five minutes of special time (Chapter 8).
- **Day 21: Check-in.** Which change helped most? Are there fewer meltdowns than in Week 1?

---

## Week 4: Teach calm and repair

*Focus: Feelings School and repair (Chapters 11 and 12)*

- **Day 22:** Teach one new feelings word and use it three times today.
- **Day 23:** Play a feelings game: charades or the inside weather report.
- **Day 24:** Practice dragon breath or blow out the candle together, for fun.
- **Day 25:** Set up a calm-down corner together.
- **Day 26:** Make your family's safe mad list if you haven't yet (Chapter 5).
- **Day 27:** If you lose your temper this week, use the repair script. If you don't, practice
  the after-the-storm talk after any meltdown.
- **Day 28:** Fill in your family's Calm-Down Plan (below) together and put it on the fridge.
- **Day 29:** Read one feelings picture book together.
- **Day 30: Final check-in.** Compare your meltdown tracker from Week 1 and Week 4.

---

## Your Day 30 check-in

Take ten minutes and answer honestly. There are no wrong answers.

1. Are meltdowns in our home **shorter, less frequent, or easier to recover from** than a
   month ago?
2. Do **I** stay calmer than I used to, at least some of the time?
3. Which **script or tool** do I use most without thinking?
4. Which moment is **still hard**? (Go back to its card in Part 2, or check Chapter 10.)
5. What's **one thing** I want to keep doing next month?

If you see even a small change, that's real progress. Children change slowly, and so do
parents. Keep the tools that work, drop the ones that don't, and come back to this book
whenever a new stage brings new storms.

And if, after a month of steady effort, meltdowns are getting worse or you're worried,
look at the signs in Chapter 10 and talk to your pediatrician. Asking for help is part of
the plan, not a failure of it.
