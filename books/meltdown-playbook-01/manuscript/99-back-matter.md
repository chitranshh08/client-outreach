# The 30 Scripts Cheat Sheet

*Short versions of every script in Part 2. Photocopy these pages or take a photo for your
phone. Always start with the reset: feet, breathe out, soft face, low voice.*

**Everyday battles (Chapter 4)**

1. **Leaving the park:** "It's hard to leave fun things. Two more slides, then shoes."
2. **Screens off:** "It's so hard to stop. Screen time is done. You press off, or should I?"
3. **Getting dressed:** "You really want to pick. Red shirt or blue shirt?"
4. **The wrong cup:** "You wanted the blue cup. So disappointing. Green or yellow?"
5. **Hearing no:** "It's hard to hear no. Cookies are after dinner. Apple or cheese?"
6. **Waiting:** "Waiting is so hard. Two more people. Hold my hand or the basket?"

**Hitting, biting and throwing (Chapter 5)** *Block first, talk later.*

7. **Hitting you:** "I won't let you hit me. You're really mad. Stomp or squeeze the pillow."
8. **Hitting another child:** *(Hurt child first.)* "I won't let you hit. Let's use words: 'Stop! That's mine!'"
9. **Biting:** "I won't let you bite. Biting hurts. You can bite this instead."
10. **Throwing:** "I won't let you throw the blocks. Soft socks in the basket, or stomp with me."

**Public meltdowns (Chapter 6)** *Turn your back to the crowd.*

11. **Checkout candy:** "It's hard to see it and not get it. No candy today. Help me with the belt?"
12. **Restaurant:** "Waiting for food is so hard. Soft voices here. Color, or a walk with me?"
13. **Playground:** "You love this playground. Time to go. Climb down, or should I help?"
14. **Family gatherings:** "So much going on today. Let's find a quiet spot together."
15. **Car seat:** "The car doesn't move until you're buckled. Climb in, or should I lift you?"

**Mornings and bedtime (Chapter 7)** *"What's next on the chart?"*

16. **Getting up:** "It's hard to leave a warm bed. Hop out like a frog or roll like a log?"
17. **Morning rush:** "It's hard to stop. Time for shoes. At the door or on the stairs?"
18. **One more story:** "That was the last story. Big hug or tiny squeeze?"
19. **"I'm not tired":** "You don't have to sleep. Your body does need to rest. Light on or off?"
20. **Night fears:** "You're scared. I'm right here, and you're safe."

**Siblings and sharing (Chapter 8)** *Sportscaster, not judge.*

21. **Toy grab:** "I won't let you grab it. You can ask, 'Can I have a turn when you're done?'"
22. **"Not fair!":** "It feels unfair. Tell me about it."
23. **Siblings hitting:** "I won't let you hit each other. You sit here, you sit there, until you're calm."
24. **New baby:** "It's hard having a new baby. I love you so much, and that will never change."

**Big feelings (Chapter 9)** *Comfort before fixing.*

25. **Losing a game:** "Losing feels so bad. Stomp it out, then play again or do something else."
26. **Broken toy:** "Oh no. You worked so hard on it. That's really sad. I'm right here."
27. **Cancelled plans:** "That's really disappointing. Let's put it on the calendar."
28. **Drop-off:** "You'll miss me. I always come back. Big hug, and then I'm going."
29. **Fears:** "You feel scared. That's okay. I'm right here, and I'll keep you safe."
30. **"I hate you!":** "You're really mad at me. I love you even when you're mad at me."

**When your mind goes blank:** "I see you're ___. I'm right here. I won't let you ___."
