## Reader avatar
- **Who:** A parent (usually mom, 25–40) of one or two kids aged 2–7.
- **Situation now:** Daily meltdowns over small things: leaving the park, the wrong cup,
  screens off, bedtime. She ends up yelling or giving in, then feels guilty.
- **Already tried:** Timeouts, counting to three, bribes, "calm down!", scrolling gentle
  parenting accounts on Instagram. The advice sounds nice but she freezes in the moment
  because she doesn't know *what to actually say*.
- **What she wants (her words):** "I just want to stop yelling." "Tell me what to say."
  "I want my kid to be able to calm down without me losing it too."

## The promise (one sentence)
> In the middle of any meltdown, you'll know exactly what to say and do to calm your
> child (and yourself) in minutes, without yelling or giving in.

## Competitor gaps → how we win
_Preliminary, from page-1 market shape. To be replaced with 1–3★ review quotes after
the review-mining pass._

| Gap (hypothesis) | Our fix |
|---|---|
| Expert books explain the *why* at length; parents want the *what to say* | Script-first: every chapter is built around word-for-word SAY THIS boxes |
| Many indie books are generic "strategies" lists that repeat online content | 30 specific real-life moments, each with a script, a "what's going on" line and a fallback |
| Advice assumes a calm parent | Chapter 1 is the parent's own 60-second reset, before any child tactic |
| One-size-fits-all across ages | Every script has age tweaks for 2–3 / 4–5 / 6–7 |
| No help after you blow it | A whole chapter on repair: what to say after you yelled |

## Positioning
*Tiny Humans, Big Emotions* and *How to Stop Losing Your Sh\*t* are excellent but read
like full courses. **The Meltdown Playbook is the in-the-moment companion**: open it,
find your situation, say the words. It sits next to them, not against them (good for
"customers also bought").

## Format & business decisions
- **Target word count:** ~30,000 (≈ 140 pages at 6×9)
- **Trim size:** 6×9 in, white paper, B&W interior
- **KDP Select:** **Yes.** 8 of the 10 page-1 books for "emotional regulation for
  parents" are in KU, and KU parents binge short practical books.
- **Launch price:** ebook $2.99 (launch 2 weeks) → $5.99 · paperback $14.99
  (page-1 paperbacks run $11.99–14.99)
- **Quick win (first 10%):** Chapter 1's "3-Line Meltdown Script" + 60-second parent
  reset, usable tonight.
- **Extras:** 30 SAY THIS script boxes, "Instead of / Try" swap tables, a one-page
  printable Calm-Down Plan, a script cheat sheet at the back, 5-minute actions per chapter.
