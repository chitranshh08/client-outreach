# Launch plan

Output: `books/<slug>/launch-plan.md` from `templates/launch-checklist.md`.

## Phases
**Pre-launch (while in KDP review, or 1 week before)**
- Recruit advance readers from the pen name's social audience. They get a free copy
  (BookFunnel/StoryOrigin or a PDF), with **no requirement to review and no requirement
  that reviews be positive**. See `docs/kdp-rules.md`.
- Draft 5–7 social posts (hook, value tip from the book, soft mention of the launch).

**Launch week**
- If in KDP Select, pick one: a 3–5 day Free Book Promotion (downloads, which bring
  reviews and algorithm signals) OR a Countdown Deal. Book 1 of a series leans free.
- Tell advance readers the book is live, and link the review page.
- Post the social content.

**Weeks 2–6: Amazon Ads test (budget-aware: ~$50–100 total per book)**
- Campaign 1: Auto targeting, low default bid, $3–5/day.
- Campaign 2: Manual keyword, 20–40 buyer phrases from the niche report.
- Campaign 3 (optional): Product targeting on competitor ASINs with weak covers or
  fewer reviews.
- After 2 weeks: pause targets with spend > one royalty and 0 sales; raise bids on
  converting ones. Track ACoS vs break-even ACoS (royalty ÷ price).

**Ongoing**
- Once 10+ reviews: add social proof lines to the description.
- Ship the next series book. Its launch lifts book 1.

## Rules
- Any money spent or anything published needs owner approval first.
- Never use review services, swaps, or incentives. Account safety beats a few reviews.
- Update the tracker: status `live`, ASINs, launch date.
