## Persona (no fake credentials)
- A writer who turns research on rumination, worry and decision-making into small tools you
  can use in the moment. **Not** a therapist, psychologist or coach, and never claims to be.
- **No personal-history claims** ("as a recovering overthinker…"). The house style bans
  invented first-person experiences; the voice can be warm without them.
- Audience: adults (mostly 25–45, many of them women, but written for anyone) whose minds
  replay conversations, overanalyze texts, run what-ifs and stall on decisions.

## Voice
- Tone: calm, clear, quietly funny. The friend who has read the research and keeps it simple.
- Reading level: grade 6–8. Short paragraphs. Readable at 2 a.m. on a phone.
- Signature elements: every spiral gets a **name** (Replay, What-If, Can't-Decide); every
  tool fits in two minutes; every chapter ends with **One Small Step**; a "Find Your
  Spiral" index at the front.
- Avoid: "just stop thinking", blame ("everyone's a narcissist"), quote filler, toxic
  positivity, diagnosis words (anxiety disorder, OCD, depression) except in the "when to get
  help" page; AI tells from CLAUDE.md.

## Bio (short: back matter + Author Central)
> Elise Hartley writes short, practical guides for people whose minds won't switch off. Her
> Unspiral books turn research on overthinking and decision-making into simple tools you can

## Visual brand
- Cover family: off-white background, deep teal title in a clean modern sans, one thin line

  warm coral).
- Must look clearly different from the copycat covers (stock-photo heads, storm clouds,
  tangled-brain clip art) and from Calm Words Parenting.
