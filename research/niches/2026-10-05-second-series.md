## Reader pain (1–3★ reviews of the top overthinking books)
1. **"Nothing new"**: the same techniques recycled from other books (Trenton).
2. **"Overly mechanical"**: a long list of techniques (23 in Trenton's book) with no guide to
   which one to use when.
3. **"Pinterest quotes cover to cover"**, fluff over substance (Chidiac).
4. **Contradictions**: "the way to not overthink is to overthink everything" (Chidiac).
5. **Blame**: "everyone around you is a narcissist or gaslighter" (Chidiac).
6. **Overreach**: advice that suggests people with anxiety can "therapize themselves" (Trenton).
7. What readers say *did* help: concrete, structured tools, e.g. a timed "worry window"
   instead of "just stop thinking."

## Recommendation: GO on a premium "stop overthinking" series

**The angle that beats page 1** (from the pain list): not another list of 23 techniques, but a
**situation-based playbook**. Find your moment (the 2 a.m. replay, the unanswered text, the
conversation you keep rehearsing, the decision you can't make), and get one short tool for that
moment plus what to do if it doesn't work. Concrete tools with real sources, no quotes-as-filler,
no blame, and a clear "when to talk to a professional" page. That's the same structure that
made Book 2 of Calm Words strong, applied to adults.

**Why it fits our zero-budget plan:** the demand is search-driven ("how to stop overthinking"
is a top autocomplete phrase), so the series can sell through Amazon search without a social
following. KU share is high (7/10), so page reads matter.

**Series map (same reader, read-through first):**
1. **Book 1:** stop overthinking: the situation playbook (main keyword)
2. **Book 2:** overthinking at night: quieting the 2 a.m. brain (autocomplete "stop
   overthinking at night"; sleep is health-adjacent, so general education only)
3. **Book 3:** overthinking in relationships: texts, silences, "what did they mean?"
   (autocomplete; weak alone, strong as read-through)
4. **Book 4:** people pleasing: saying no without the guilt spiral (weak alone, good fit for
   the same reader)
5. Later: letting go of the past · a 3-book bundle

**Pricing hypothesis (decide at blueprint):** $4.99–6.99 ebook, $14.99 paperback. Don't
compete with the $0.99 clones; they're the ones losing.

## Flags for the owner (CLAUDE.md rule 4)
- **Mental-health adjacency:** overthinking overlaps with anxiety. The book must stay general
  education: no diagnosis, no "cure anxiety" claims, a clear disclaimer, and signs it's time to
  see a professional. Same standard as Calm Words' pediatrician pages.
- **New pen name needed** (separate brand bible in `pen-names/`), with no fake credentials.
- **No trademarked phrases** ("Setting Boundaries®") and no other authors' names in metadata.
