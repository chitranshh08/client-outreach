### What the evidence says
1. **Demand is real and lasting.** Four traditional books on this exact problem sit in the top
   40 of big Kindle parenting categories decades after release.
2. **Indie books can win, and they win as series.** The two indie books ranking well (Khang,
   Carter) are both Book 1 of a branded parenting series with a clear promise and a strong cover.
3. **Child Discipline is a small category with a low bar.** #1 ≈ #43.6k BSR and #3 ≈ #62k
   (≈ 2–5 sales/day). A modest launch (ARC readers + Book 1's audience + a small ads test)
   can put Book 2 in the top 3 of Child Discipline, which earns the category badge and
   visibility.
4. **Clones fail.** 2026's flood of "X Scripts / X Playbook" titles sits at 1.7M–4.5M.
   Volume of new titles isn't competition; quality and series are what sell. It does mean
   our title and cover must stand apart from that wave.
5. **Price anchor is low for indies** ($3.99 + KU for Carter). Our planned $5.99 is
   defensible with a stronger book and matches Book 1; launch at $2.99.
